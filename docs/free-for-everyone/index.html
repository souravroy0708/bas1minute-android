Home/Free for everyone

Free for as many people as possible.

Our mission is to help India get fit, starting with people who say they have no time. A price tag would stop the people who need it most, so the core app is free.

₹0for the core app

What’s free

Everything you need to build the 7K + 4 habit is in the free core. No trial that runs out, no locked basics.

  • The 7K + 4 daily ring
  • All launch moments: morning gate, sitting alert, Walk & Talk, chai burst
  • Camera rep counting, on your phone
  • A private family circle for a few members
  • The monthly fitness age test
  • Hindi and English, with voice prompts
  • Gentle mode for people who need lower-intensity movement

Why we can keep it free

Free only works if running the app costs very little. We designed for that from the first line of code.

Your phone does the work

Step counting, activity detection and rep counting all run on sensors your phone already has.

No AI bill

No AI model in the loop, so every extra user costs us roughly a rupee a month on servers.

No paid installs

We grow through family invites and word of mouth, not by buying downloads.

A small team

We keep the team small until the pilot proves people keep the habit.

Who pays instead

Organisations that benefit from healthier people fund the app, so individuals don’t have to.

  • Workplaces

    Companies pay for wellness programmes for their staff, a small amount per employee.

  • Brands

    Sportswear, health-food and insurance brands sponsor seasonal challenges like IPL and Navratri.

  • Housing societies

    Resident welfare associations run building-wide challenges.

  • Clinics

    Diabetes clinics and doctors use it with prediabetic patients, once the evidence is in.

  • Families who want more

    An optional family plan for bigger circles. The core never needs it.

Our promises

  1. The core stays free.

    Anything needed to build the daily habit will never move behind a paywall.

  2. We don’t sell your data.

    Sponsors and employers see totals, like how many people finished a challenge. Never your name or your activity.

  3. Your camera stays yours.

    Rep counting happens on your phone. The video never leaves it.

  4. Safety is never a paid extra.

    Gentle mode and safety questions are part of the free app.
