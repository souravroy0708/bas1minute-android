Busy people don’t need an hour.

Half of Indian adults don’t move enough, and most say time is the reason. So we stopped asking for time. Bas 1 Minute fits fitness inside the day you already have.

One daily target: 7K + 4

Two small numbers that carry most of the benefit and fit inside a working day. A daily on-ramp toward the WHO’s 150 minutes of activity a week, with no gym.

7,000

steps a day

Walking spread through the day, not one long walk. Linked to a 47% lower risk of death from any cause compared with 2,000 steps. Even 4,000 steps is linked to a 36% lower risk.

Lancet Public Health, 2025

4

one-minute bursts

Stairs taken fast, a brisk walk to the bus, 20 quick squats. Three to four a day are linked to up to 40% fewer cancer deaths and up to 49% fewer heart-disease deaths.

Nature Medicine

These studies show associations, not guarantees. The app will always say “linked to”, never “will add years”.

The moments engine

The app never asks for a workout slot. It attaches a burst or a few hundred steps to moments you already live through. You choose which ones, and it never sends more than three nudges a day.

  • 7:00 am At launch

    Morning gate

    Instagram and YouTube wait until your first burst. The camera counts 20 squats, and you’re in.

  • 11:00 am At launch

    Sitting alert

    Still for an hour? One minute on the stairs or a quick squat set, right where you are.

  • Any call At launch

    Walk & Talk

    Pace while you talk. Every step on a call counts toward your 7,000.

  • 4:00 pm At launch

    Chai burst

    Chai ban rahi hai? Twenty squats while the water boils.

  • 9:00 am Coming later

    Ek Stop Pehle

    Your phone buzzes one metro or bus stop early, so you walk the rest.

  • Evening Coming later

    Best walking hour

    The cleanest-air or coolest hour for a walk, or an indoor routine when it’s bad out.

  • After 10 pm Coming later

    Reels surge pricing

    Late-night Reels cost a few more reps. Scrolling stays possible; it just asks for movement first.

  • Weekends Coming later

    Family & festival challenges

    Family goals plus IPL, wedding-season and Navratri challenges.

Built to survive week three

Most fitness apps lose people after a few weeks. Every mechanic here either lowers the effort to start or raises the cost of quietly stopping.

Start tiny

Week one asks for a single burst a day. The target grows on its own.

Removes the “no time” excuse on day one.

Fitness age

A monthly six-minute walk test turns into a number: your body’s age versus yours.

A personal health check you can see change.

Family circle

Parents, spouse and siblings see each other’s 7K + 4 ring.

Accountability from people who matter, not strangers.

Workout debt

Miss a day and the reps carry over as a small debt.

No “streak broken, I quit” spiral.

Seasonal challenges

IPL, wedding season, Navratri, New Year.

Fresh reasons to come back through the year.

Designed for women from day one

In 2024, 4.5% of married women in India exercised, against 17.3% of men. Women named unstructured days, stray dogs, narrow roads and no habit of walking as barriers. The first version has to work for a woman with no free hour and no safe place to walk.

  • Home-first bursts. Every moment has a version that needs no trip outside.
  • Your own timing. Bursts count any time before midnight. No class times.
  • Women-only circles. Friends, sisters or neighbours instead of a public feed.
  • Fitness age, gently. Shows whether daily chores alone are moving the number.
  • Safe walking windows. Daylight and best-air hours suggested for outdoor walks.
