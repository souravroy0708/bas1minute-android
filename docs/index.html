No workout slot. Just one minute at a time.

The app fits fitness inside your day instead of on top of it.

  1. Pick your moments

    Waking up, sitting too long, on calls, at chai time. You choose, and it never sends more than three nudges a day.

  2. Move for one minute

    Twenty squats, fast stairs or a brisk walk. Your phone counts steps and reps. No wearable needed.

  3. Close your 7K + 4 ring

    Your family sees you moved today, and the target grows gently as you do.

Family circle

Parents, spouse and siblings share one private ring view. Accountability from people who matter, not strangers. Miss a day and the reps carry over as a small workout debt, so one bad day never resets everything.

Fitness age

A number that shows your effort working

Once a month, a six-minute walk test turns into your body’s fitness age. It’s a gentle check you can watch change, and it shows whether daily chores alone are moving the number.

  • Measured with your phone, no equipment
  • Gentle mode for lower-intensity movement

Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Write to us.

Do I need a gym or equipment?

No. Every burst works at home, at your desk or on the stairs.

Is it really free?

Yes, the core app is free. Here’s how we keep it that way.

Who can see my activity?

Only people you invite to your circle. When the camera counts your reps, the video stays on your phone.

What if I have a heart condition?

A few questions at sign-up switch you to a gentle mode with brisk walking instead of bursts. Please check with your doctor before you start.

When does it launch, and is there an iPhone app?

The Android app opens on 24 November 2026. iPhone will follow later.
