Privacy policy

For the Bas 1 Minute Android app (com.bas1minute.sync) and this website. Last updated 13 November 2026.

Who we are

Bas 1 Minute is made by Sourav Roy in Hyderabad, India, as individual developers rather than a company. Data fiduciary for the data described here: Sourav Roy. Contact: tosourav6@gmail.com.

The short version

The app works fully offline, and your whole history is kept on the phone, in its own database, for as long as you keep the app. Backup, Circles and the Bas 1 Minute India ranking are on when you start: the app tells you so on the name screen, and the last setup screen shows each one with its own switch so you can turn it off before you begin. While they’re on, our server receives your name, the goals you set, and your daily step total and daily social-media screen-time total. Turn any of them off at any time in Profile; with “Online features” off, nothing leaves your phone. Separately, your Goals, Life goals, Diary and Digital detox history stay on your phone unless you turn on a second, off-by-default setting to sync them to this website — see "Syncing Goals, Diary and Digital detox" below. We don’t sell data, show ads or use analytics or advertising SDKs.

What stays on your phone

These are stored only on your device and are never sent to us, however your sharing and syncing settings are set: sleep, water and weight (read from Health Connect or typed by you), walks, runs and rides you record (with GPS, location is used only while one you started is being tracked; without GPS, distance comes from your step counter), mood, calm minutes, and one-minute bursts. They are removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Phone check results and per-app usage stay on your phone too. So do your daily Goals, your Life goals, your Diary, and your Digital detox sessions and clean hours — unless you turn on "Sync my goals, diary and detox to the website" in Profile, which is off by default; see the next section for exactly what that sends.

What we receive and store

  • Account: the name you choose, your city if you add it, and your step and screen-time goals. No phone number, email address or password is needed.
  • Device details: a keyed hash of a random install ID (so reinstalling gives you back the same account), a sign-in token, the phone model, and a Firebase Cloud Messaging token used to send notifications.
  • Shared activity (on when you join from the app, off any time): while sharing is on, the app sends one step total and one social-media screen-time total per day, with whether each came from Health Connect, the phone’s step sensor or Android usage statistics. Never app names, times of use, location, contacts or messages.
  • Circles and competitions: circles you create or join, their names and descriptions, join-request notes, cheers, reports you file, and competition entries.
  • Website sign-in: when you approve a website sign-in from the app, we keep the browser’s IP address and user agent so you can see and sign out signed-in browsers.
  • Waitlist (website only): name, email or WhatsApp number, city, pilot interest and referral code.

Health Connect

With your permission the app reads Steps, Sleep, Hydration and Weight from Health Connect, including in the background so your totals and widget stay up to date. Only the daily step total can leave your phone, and only when sharing is on. We use Health Connect data only to show your progress and run circle and competition scoring you take part in. We don’t use it for advertising, don’t sell it, and don’t transfer it to anyone except as needed to provide these features, to follow the law, or as part of a merger. People do not read it except with your consent, for security, or where the law requires. The app’s use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Usage access

If you grant Android’s Usage access, the app adds up how long you spent in social-media apps each day and runs Phone check on your phone. Only the daily social total can be shared, and only when sharing is on.

Digital detox

Digital detox, Schedules and App Timers need only Android’s Usage access, which tells the app which app is open and for how long — never what’s inside it. The app never asks for Accessibility access. By default it doesn’t ask for “Display over other apps” or run in the background all the time either. Only if you turn on Strict mode for a detox does it ask for “Display over other apps”, so it can cover an app you chose to pause with a countdown screen; it then runs a small service with a visible countdown notification for that detox only, checks which app is open only while your screen is on, and stops when the time is up. During a detox it checks every few minutes; if an app you chose to pause is open, it sends you a reminder notification, and when the time is up it checks Android’s own usage history to mark the promise kept or broken. App Timers check about every 15 minutes and remind you once an app’s daily time is spent. If you want an app locked so it can’t be opened, the app opens Android’s own Digital Wellbeing for you to set that up yourself; Bas 1 Minute gets no extra access from it. The record of kept and broken promises stays on your phone. Nothing about which apps you opened is ever sent to us — we only ever receive the session’s length, start and end time, outcome (kept or guilty) and, if it broke, which app broke it (as an app name, never a screenshot or usage log), and only if you’ve turned on syncing.

Syncing Goals, Diary and Digital detox

Goals, Life goals, Diary and Digital detox are the most personal things in the app — things like a daughter's date of birth, a daily reflection, or a photo of who a goal is for. They stay on your phone unless you turn on "Sync my goals, diary and detox to the website" in Profile (or on the sync settings page on this site); it is off for every member by default and stays off until you turn it on yourself.

Turning it on sends: the title, target, dates and daily check-offs of each daily goal; the horizon, plan, why, obstacle, plan-for-it, target date and milestones of each life goal, and any photo you added to one; the date, category, title, story, and effort/satisfaction ratings of each diary entry, and any photo you added to one; and each digital detox session's length, outcome and which app broke it, if any.

This also doubles as a backup: if you uninstall the app and later reinstall it on the same phone, the app recognises it and offers to bring this data straight back, exactly as it was. It only ever offers this when the app's own storage on that phone is completely empty, so there's no risk of it duplicating or overwriting anything already there. A different phone, or this phone after Android's own reset, is not recognised, and nothing is restored.

Photos are stored on our server but shown only to you, through a page that checks you're signed in as their owner — never a public link, never shown to other members, and never part of circles, competitions or the global ranking. This part of the website is read-only: you add and change things in the app, and the website mirrors what's been synced.

Turning sync off stops sending anything new; what's already on the server stays until you delete it (Profile → Delete my data, or the account deletion steps below delete this too).

Exporting your data

“Export my data” in the app makes a file of everything saved on your phone for the dates you pick — daily steps, screen time and time per app, sleep, water, weight, walks (distance and time only, never GPS points), detox sessions and goals, and your diary only if you tick it. The file is made on your phone and goes only where you send it, for example to an AI app such as Claude or ChatGPT, or to a spreadsheet. Once you send it, that service’s own privacy policy applies. We never receive it.

Protections built into the app

Goals, Life goals and Diary are shielded by default: they can’t be screenshotted or screen-recorded, and they show as a blank card in your phone’s Recent apps (you can turn this off in Profile). With App lock on, the whole app shows blank in Recent apps. Android’s Google Drive backup and phone-to-phone transfer are switched off for this app, so your data isn’t copied anywhere you didn’t choose. The app has no analytics or advertising SDKs and never collects your advertising ID. Firebase is used only to deliver notifications, and the app doesn’t request a notification token unless notifications are on. Voice typing in Diary and Goals is optional: the app asks for the microphone only the first time you tap a mic, uses it only while the Listening window is open, and turns your words into text with your phone’s own speech service (on the phone itself where your phone supports it). Audio is never saved and never sent to us; only the text appears, and you can edit it before saving.

Who sees your data

Members of circles you join see your name and daily step progress; your screen time only in circles where you switch it on. If you appear in the global ranking or a competition, other members see your name, city (if set) and score. Everyone who shares is in the global ranking and in monthly challenges automatically. You can leave the ranking, or any single challenge, at any time in the app and rejoin later. Your own website dashboard shows only what your phone shared.

Service providers who process data for us: PythonAnywhere (server hosting) and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications). All traffic uses HTTPS.

How long we keep it

Your shared daily step and social-media totals are kept for the last 92 days only; older days are reduced to running totals (all-time steps and goal days) for the rankings. Your phone keeps your full day-by-day history. Everything else is kept until you delete it. If every phone on your account has uninstalled the app, the account is hidden right away and deleted after 30 days, including anything synced under "Syncing Goals, Diary and Digital detox" above. Server logs expire within 30 days. Waitlist entries are deleted after launch or on request.

Your choices and rights

Turn sharing, syncing or both off at any time; delete only your shared or synced data, or your whole account, from the app (Profile → Your data) or the website. Without the app, follow these steps or email us. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act you can ask to access, correct or erase your data; we answer within 7 days.

Children

Bas 1 Minute is meant for people aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from children; if you think a child has an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

We’ll update the date above and tell you in the app before any change that affects how your data is used.
